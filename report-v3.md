# Re-cut v3 — Cafe Cursor Shanghai recap (2026-11-13)

Two versions of the same 21-pick cut, rendered from Inkling 2 Small's picks.
Both share the same video structure, subtitles and audio mix; they differ in
grade and music only.

## What changed vs v2

- **Hook first.** The cut opens on Talk L, the room laughing together (clip
  0051, 9 s) — the emotional peak — before any wide opening shot.
- **Fast flashback.** Right after the hook, five 2 s flashes (storefront,
  setup, cups, screen, audience) before the main flow settles.
- **Organized by space.** Exterior/street first (banner, street, walking in),
  then the full room, then the talks, then Q&A, then the sign-off at the
  banner. The cut moves through the space the way a guest would.
- **Pink/blonde woman removed.** Every segment showing the woman with
  pink-dyed blonde hair was excluded. See "Exclusions" below.
- **More women, more exterior.** More women presenters and audience members
  in frame; more outside/street shots showing the whole streetscape.
- **Cursor logo prominent.** Three picks show the black Cursor banner with a
  readable logo (see "Logo visibility").
- **Bilingual outline subtitles.** Every segment carries a one-line outline
  (what is happening), in English + Chinese, in a handwritten font with a
  semi-transparent dark box. No longer just the pick title.
- **Two grades, two music tracks.** See the version table below.
- **Louder audio.** Room audio 1.4 (voices forward), music 0.30 (audible but
  under the voices).
- **No guitar / music moment.** Confirmed absent from the morning footage
  (all 17 talks are AI tools/agents/apps/code). It is afternoon material;
  not invented, not included.

## Exclusions (pink/blonde woman)

The user asked to remove every segment showing a woman with pink-dyed blonde
hair (a blonde woman in a pink top). Two exclusions were applied:

1. **Clips 0026 and 0052 were never picked.** Inkling's prompt listed them as
   excluded, and Inkling did not pick either. Clip 0052 ("full room before
   noon") was the user's requested full-room shot, but it shows the
   pink-blonde woman in the foreground for its whole duration, so the removal
   takes precedence. Clip 0020 (pick #10) is the substitute full-room shot.
2. **Pick #7 was corrected after Inkling returned it.** Inkling originally
   picked clip 0042 (10:18, storefront) for the opening wide shot. On visual
   inspection, the blonde woman in a pink top is in the foreground of that
   clip for the whole 0:02–0:07 range, so the pick was replaced with clip
   0032 (hosts thumbs up at the Cursor banner, 0:00–0:05, same 5 s duration,
   banner logo clearly readable, no excluded person). This is a correction,
   not a re-pick. The total pick duration is unchanged (118.0 s).

No other v3 pick shows the pink/blonde woman. Verified by visual inspection
of representative frames for all 21 picks.
